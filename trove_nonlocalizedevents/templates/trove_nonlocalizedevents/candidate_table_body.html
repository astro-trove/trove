Photometry Scoring Method

The candidate score is a product of subscores, one of which comes from the light curve. This chooses how that factor is computed. It applies site-wide, to everyone.

{% bootstrap_field phot_scoring_form.phot_method %} {# initial visibility is rendered server-side from the current method, #} {# so the fields for the method you are NOT on are never briefly shown #} {# and the modal is still correct if the toggle script has not run #}
Checks the fitted peak luminosity, time of peak and decay rate against the allowed ranges for the transient class. In range scores 1, out of range scores 0.1. Nothing is re-computed — the ranking updates immediately.
Applying this re-scores every candidate of this event with KilonovaSCORER. It runs in the background and takes minutes to hours for a large event. Candidates keep their previous score until the new one lands; ones that cannot be scored (too sparse, no usable distance, no band in common with the grid) are reported with a reason on their score-details page and ranked as if out of range.
